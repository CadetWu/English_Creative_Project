Captain Valmore Bourque

Welcome to Bourque's Virtual Museum

Valmore Bourque was the first cadet to be enrolled at USAFA and is remembered as the first USAFA graduate to lose his life in service. Upon graduation, Bourque went on to fly both the C-131 and C-123. On October 24, 1964, Bourque's C-123B was on an aerial resupply mission to a U.S. Special Forces camp at Bu Prang, near the Cambodian border. Unfortunately, Bourque's aircraft mistakenly went over the Cambodian camp of Dak Dam and was struck by ground fire. His legacy endures at USAFA as an example of duty, courage, and commitment to country.

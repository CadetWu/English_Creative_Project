First Lieutenant Karl W. Richter

Welcome to Richter's Virtual Museum

Karl W. Richter was a 1964 USAFA Grad known for being the youngest pilot to shoot down a MIG. Richter flew over 100 combat missions, demonstrating exceptional skill and courage under intense conditions. In July 1967, during a strike mission over North Vietnam, his aircraft was hit by enemy fire and crashed. He was killed in action, leaving behind a legacy as one of the Air Force’s most accomplished young pilots.
